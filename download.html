Questions

Good to know.

Is Sonora really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence. There is no price and no account.

Which APK should I pick?

Most phones need arm64-v8a. The releases page lists the other builds, for armeabi-v7a and x86_64 devices.

Does it need internet?

No. Sonora plays files from your phone. The app can check GitHub for new releases, but playback never depends on it.

What happens to my music files?

Nothing. Sonora reads them where they are. It does not copy or upload them.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes, but it is not on the App Store. Every release includes an unsigned IPA that you sign and install yourself with a sideloading tool. It has only been tested on a simulator so far. See the iPhone guide.
