Accessibility

Music should be for everyone. So should this website.

What we do

  • Every page works with a keyboard, and the focus is always visible.
  • Text has a contrast of at least 4.5:1.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44px tall on phones.
  • Pages work on a 390px wide phone without sideways scrolling.
  • Animations are small, and they stop if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Form errors are written out in words, not shown by colour alone.
  • Images have a text description.

Where we stand

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. We have not had the site audited by a third party, so some problems may remain.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, write to emreyurtseven18@gmail.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong. We will try to fix it.
