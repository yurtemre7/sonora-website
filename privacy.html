Privacy

This page explains what happens to your data on this website.

Who is responsible

Emre Yurtseven, Innsbrucker Str. 47A, 10825 Berlin, Deutschland. Email: emreyurtseven18@gmail.com.

No tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Netlify's servers may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Contact form

If you send the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message through Netlify Forms. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them when the conversation is finished.

Latest version on the download page

The download page asks GitHub for the number of the latest release. Your browser sends this request to GitHub directly, so GitHub can see your IP address. GitHub's own privacy policy applies.

Links to other sites

The download and source code links lead to GitHub. GitHub's own privacy policy applies there.

The app

Sonora plays music files from your phone. It does not copy or upload them.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of your data at any time by email.
